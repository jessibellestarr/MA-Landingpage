# Funeral Arrangements Workbook — product proposal

Draft, October 11, 2026. Not a product currently offered for sale.

## Recommendation

Keep the state guides, federal rights, essential questions, checklist, and manual estimate comparison free. Offer an optional workbook for people who want their calls, quotes, decisions, and follow-up in one place. Charge for a useful finished document, original worksheets, and optional printing—not access to rights or a claim that a bill is compliant.

Test $9 for a downloadable, fillable and printable PDF. This is a proposed test price, not evidence of demand or profitability. No subscription, account requirement, upsell during a crisis, or promise of savings. Include a representative free sample before purchase. Do not launch a checkout until the file, delivery, support contact, and refund terms work.

## What makes this useful

Focus on arranging a funeral: questions to ask now, apples-to-apples quotes, unknown costs, who will confirm what, and records of decisions. Planning-ahead pages should help record preferences without implying they create a legally binding appointment or authorization. Avoid turning this into an estate-planning binder collecting account numbers and passwords.

Working title: **Funeral Arrangements Workbook: Questions, Quotes, and Decisions**.

Proposed 24-page edition:

1. Cover and edition date.
2. How to use it, privacy and scope.
3–4. Quick-start questions and contact sheet (also free).
5–6. Preferences, budget, and decision-making authority to confirm.
7–9. Three repeatable provider call sheets.
10–12. Three itemized quote worksheets with included / additional / unknown fields.
13–14. Side-by-side comparison and package double-counting check.
15. Law, provider policy, cemetery/crematory rule, or recommendation?
16. Transport, release, and timing questions.
17. Cemetery and crematory questions.
18. Decision record and unresolved items.
19. Before signing: statement, permissions, changes, and payment questions.
20. Receipts and follow-up log.
21. Planning ahead: where wishes are kept and who knows.
22. Prepayment questions: guarantees, funding, cancellation, moving, closure.
23. Concerns: factual timeline, documents, and official contacts.
24. Sources, edition date, and where to find updated free state guides.

The starter manuscript in WORKBOOK-DRAFT.md contains original worksheet prompts. Expand and lay it out before treating it as the final 24-page product. A fillable PDF and paper proof do not exist yet.

## Paper edition

Evaluate a lay-flat coil-bound edition and a lower-cost paperback. Print-on-demand can avoid inventory, but compare delivered cost and actual production time. A physical copy is better suited to planning ahead; people arranging a funeral now should have an immediately accessible digital option.

Do not advertise a print price yet. Obtain a quote for the final trim size, page count, paper, binding, and delivery destination. Calculate contribution after printing, shipping, fulfillment, payment/platform fees, applicable taxes, and a returns/replacement allowance. Order and inspect one proof for writing space, grayscale contrast, margins, and binding clearance. Only publish delivery estimates supplied for the actual fulfillment setup.

Lulu Direct is one candidate, not a selected vendor. Its official documentation lists printing, shipping/handling, fulfillment fees, and applicable taxes; its example is not a quote for this workbook.

## Market reference

Funeral Consumers Alliance lists its 37-page spiral-bound “Before I Go, You Should Know” workbook at $20 including U.S. shipping ($15 product + $5 shipping/handling). It covers broader end-of-life wishes and instructions. This is one observed comparison, not proof of willingness to pay for our product. Our proposed emphasis is the practical work of collecting and comparing funeral arrangements, using original content.
Source, checked October 11, 2026: https://www.funerals.org/product/before-i-go-end-of-life-planner-spiral-bound/

## Honest sales copy for a future launch

“Keep your funeral questions, provider quotes, and decisions together. This optional workbook includes reusable call sheets, comparison worksheets, and space for notes. Our rights information, state guides, and basic comparison tool remain free.”

Only add “fillable,” “instant download,” a page count, or a shipping estimate once delivered functionality matches the description. Avoid “save thousands,” “never overpay,” “complete legal protection,” “certified compliant,” “all state laws,” or lifetime-update promises. State plainly that it does not appoint a legal decision maker, authorize disposition, or determine legal compliance.

## Small launch test

Finish and proof the PDF, let a few willing readers try it, correct confusing fields, then offer the digital edition at the proposed price. Measure purchases, refund requests, and whether readers can complete a comparison. No revenue forecast is justified yet. Add print only after costs and fulfillment are verified. No payment integration or outreach was performed during this review.
