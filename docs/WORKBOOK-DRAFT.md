# Funeral Arrangements Workbook
## Questions, Quotes, and Decisions

**Working manuscript — October 11, 2026. Not the finished paid product.**

A place to organize questions and answers while arranging a funeral or planning ahead. Leave any question blank until you have an answer. “Unknown” is useful information; it does not mean zero cost.

This workbook is educational. It does not appoint a decision maker, authorize services, settle family disagreements, determine compliance, or guarantee savings. Use the linked official sources to confirm requirements for your circumstances. Keep your completed copy somewhere private. Record where important documents are kept rather than copying identification numbers, passwords, or financial account details here.

### Planning ahead without assuming prepayment

Preferences I want people to know: ______.

Who knows where these notes are kept? ______. Review date: ______.

Before buying a prepaid arrangement, ask:

- Who holds the money, or what insurance funds the arrangement?
- Which exact prices and services are guaranteed? Which are not?
- What happens if I cancel, move, or choose another provider?
- What happens if the provider closes?
- Who regulates this seller and funding arrangement?
- What written documents will I receive?

Answers and documents to review: ______. Planning preferences and paying in advance are separate decisions. Confirm state-specific contract and authority requirements with official sources.
