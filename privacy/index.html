Privacy

What this site does and doesn’t collect.

Morticians Audit is a static website. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics scripts, and no document uploads.

The two starting questions

When you answer the questions on the home page, your choices are added to the address of the next page so you can come back to it. Our hosting provider may record requested addresses in routine server logs, which means those choices can appear there. We don’t ask for your name or any details about the person who died.

The comparison worksheet

Amounts you type into the estimate comparison stay in your browser’s memory on that page. They are not sent to us and disappear when you reload.

If you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you write. Please keep messages general. Don’t send death certificates, medical records, financial account details, or other sensitive documents. Email is not a secure way to share them.

Links to other sites

Linked government and other websites have their own privacy practices.

Changes

If this site starts collecting anything new, this page will be updated first. Page updated October 5, 2026.
