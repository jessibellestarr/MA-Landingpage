Who can authorize arrangements?

Florida law defines a “legally authorized person” in a priority order. It begins with the decedent when valid written directions exist, then certain military designees, then the surviving spouse, adult child, parent, adult sibling, adult grandchild, grandparent, and next degree of kinship. Additional people may qualify when no listed family member exists or is available.

Source: Fla. Stat. § 497.005(43).

Refrigeration or preservation after 24 hours

Florida generally does not allow a dead human body to be held in a place or in transit for more than 24 hours after death or pending final disposition unless the body is refrigerated at 40°F or below, embalmed, or otherwise preserved in an approved manner. That rule is not the same thing as a universal requirement to embalm.

Source: Fla. Stat. § 497.386.

Cremation authorization

A Florida cremation may not be performed until a legally authorized person gives written authorization. The person arranging the cremation also designates intentions for the cremated remains in a signed declaration. Florida law further states that cremation must be performed within 48 hours after a specified time agreed to in writing by the authorizing person.

Source: Fla. Stat. § 497.607.

Transport

Florida requires a body transported by common carrier or another authorized carrier to be placed in a carrying container adequate to prevent fluid seepage and offensive odors. Crossing state lines may add requirements at both ends, so confirm the route before collection.

Source: Fla. Stat. § 497.386.

Planning ahead

Write down your wishes and who should act. Recording preferences and buying a prepaid contract are separate choices. Florida regulates preneed sales under Chapter 497; ask what is guaranteed, how funds or insurance are held, and what happens if you cancel or move.

Source: Florida Statutes, Chapter 497.

Words you can use

Please show me the written requirement behind that charge. Is it a law, cemetery rule, crematory rule, or provider policy?

Please quote the complete total for the arrangement I described, including refrigeration or other preservation, transport, permits, and third-party charges.

Where to take a concern

Florida funeral, cemetery, cremation, and preneed regulation is governed by Chapter 497 and the Board of Funeral, Cemetery, and Consumer Services. Keep the GPL, itemized statement, receipts, authorizations, and messages when raising a concern.
