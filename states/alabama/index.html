State funeral prices and rights

Alabama

Practical questions about authority, cremation, care, and written prices.

Sources reviewed October 10, 2026. This guide covers selected state requirements. It does not resolve every preservation, transport, cemetery, home-burial, or medical-examiner question.

A death has happened

  1. Confirm who can authorize arrangements and whether a medical-examiner or other hold prevents release.
  2. Ask for the General Price List and an itemized total for the arrangement you want.
  3. Ask about storage charges, permits, third-party charges, and the expected timetable.

Alabama statutory summaries below use the Board’s October 2023 compilation. Confirm subsequent amendments with the Board for your arrangement.

Preservation and public viewing

Under § 34-13-117, disposition must occur within 48 hours of death or release by the coroner or medical examiner unless the body is refrigerated or embalmed by an Alabama-licensed embalmer with the authorizing agent’s permission. Public viewing of an unembalmed body is prohibited after 24 hours from death. That viewing restriction is different from a universal embalming requirement.

Source: Board-published statutes, § 34-13-117.

Cremation timing and paperwork

Section 34-13-121 generally prohibits cremation within 24 hours of death, with a narrow disease-related waiver. It requires a signed Board-approved authorization and state identification form. Cremations must be arranged through a licensed funeral establishment. A crematory unable or unauthorized to cremate immediately must refrigerate the remains.

Source: Board-published statutes, § 34-13-121.

Planning ahead

Record your wishes and check the formal requirements for choosing a decision maker. Planning and paying in advance are separate decisions. Before buying a prepaid contract, ask who holds the money, which prices are guaranteed, and how cancellation, relocation, and provider closure are handled. Confirm the seller’s licensing and contract oversight with the relevant regulator.

Transport and questions this guide does not settle

Before collecting or transporting remains, confirm release authority, permits, preservation, container requirements, and acceptance at the destination. Crossing state lines can involve rules at both ends. This guide does not establish permission for family transport, home burial, or a particular route.

Words you can use

Please show me the written requirement behind that charge. Is it a law, cemetery rule, crematory rule, or provider policy?

Please quote the total, including transport, storage, permits, and third-party charges, and tell me what could change it.

Where to take a concern

The Alabama Board of Funeral Service accepts complaints about alleged violations by licensees. Its complaint page provides online and printable forms. Include dates, the provider’s name, and copies of supporting documents.

Alabama Board complaint process · FTC reporting portal

Keep the price lists, itemized statement, receipts, authorizations, and messages. A high price by itself does not prove misconduct, and a complaint does not guarantee a refund.
