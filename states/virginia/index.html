Virginia funeral prices and rights

Virginia

Start with the next decision. Ask for prices, confirm who can authorize arrangements, and separate a legal requirement from a provider policy.

Sources reviewed October 10, 2026. Covers selected state requirements, not every funeral, cemetery, transport, or public-health rule.

A death has happened

  1. Confirm who has authority to make arrangements and whether a medical-examiner or other hold applies.
  2. Ask for the General Price List and a written, itemized total before agreeing.
  3. Keep every authorization and ask what law, cemetery rule, crematory rule, or provider policy supports anything presented as mandatory.

Who can make the decisions?

Virginia lets a person designate someone in a signed and notarized writing, accepted in writing by the designee, to arrange the funeral and disposition. That designation can take priority when the required copy is provided on time. Virginia law also recognizes other decision-makers in specified circumstances, including certain advance-directive agents and guardians.

Source: Va. Code § 54.1-2825; Va. Code § 32.1-309.1.

Refrigeration and embalming permission

If a body will be stored for more than 48 hours before disposition, a Virginia funeral establishment must ensure that it is refrigerated at no more than approximately 40°F or embalmed. In the absence of express permission from next of kin or a court order, the body must be refrigerated rather than embalmed.

Virginia regulations describe express permission to embalm as written authorization or direct verbal authorization followed as soon as possible by signed written confirmation.

Sources: Va. Code § 54.1-2811.1; 18VAC65-20-510.

Cremation requires more than a family decision

Virginia requires permission from the Office of the Chief Medical Examiner before cremation and requires visual identification by next of kin or another qualifying representative, with alternate identification rules when that person fails or refuses to identify the deceased.

Source: Va. Code § 54.1-2818.1.

Planning ahead

Write down your wishes and who should act. Recording preferences and buying a prepaid contract are separate decisions. If you prepay, ask what is guaranteed, who holds the money or policy, and what happens if you move, cancel, or the provider closes.

Words you can use

Please show me the written requirement behind that charge. Is it a law, a cemetery rule, a crematory rule, or your policy?

Please give me the complete itemized total, including transport, storage, permits, and third-party charges.

Where to take a concern

The Virginia Department of Health Professions Enforcement Division receives complaints involving funeral establishments regulated by the Board of Funeral Directors and Embalmers. The agency states that the boards cannot order a licensee to return money or provide other personal remedies.

Virginia DHP Enforcement · complaints: 1-800-533-1560.

For a possible federal Funeral Rule violation, report it to the FTC.
